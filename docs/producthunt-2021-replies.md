# Replies to 2021 Product Hunt commenters, for launch day

Post these on launch day as replies on the 2021 post (https://www.producthunt.com/posts/giftpicker-by-presently), once the new launch is live, so each person gets a reply notification. Links use https://giftpicker.io/ph, which redirects to the 2026 launch page. PH shortens any URL over about 30 characters to "https://www.producthunt.com/prod..." in comments and in its notification emails, so the long PH link arrived cut off in people's inboxes (reported 10/13). The first 18 replies went out with the long link. After the emails had gone out, Dalia asked (10/13) to switch the 31 short-link replies back to the long link, so they were edited to the launch page link (/launches/giftpicker); the first 18 keep ?launch=giftpicker. Bhaumik's review reply was rejected by PH ("Comment invalid content detected") and left unposted. Team members Alex, Nicholas, Spencer, and Qali also got personal thank-you replies with the launch page link (texts approved by Dalia; Qali's credits her as the original builder). Short and excited, in the style Dalia picked (10/12): only Nate and Anup get a callout, for the features they asked for; everyone else gets one of five short variants. None asks for an upvote: Product Hunt removes or demotes launches that ask for upvotes or mass-message users.

Covers all eight comment pages of the 2021 thread (read 10/12/26), 50 people in all. Team members from 2021 are left out: Alex Reed, Nicholas Osborne, Vrushali Tarte, Spencer Belsky, and the interns Dalia tagged (Marc Bacvanski, Erin Kim, nsortur). Qali Langstaff, also marked Maker, gets a personal note from you rather than a template.

Pacing: 50 replies posted back to back can read as mass messaging, which Product Hunt penalizes. Spread them through launch day in small batches, starting with the people who asked for something (Nate, Anup) or said they would use it, and edit any line so it sounds like you.
